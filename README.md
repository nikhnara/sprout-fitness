# Gamna

**Make room for you.** A personal food, movement and progress journal.

[Open Gamna](https://gamna.app)

## Your everyday companion

- Sign in with Google to open your private diary.
- Build a plate from a searchable food library, edit portions, and save your own recipes.
- Track calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, fiber, weight, sleep and activity.
- Explore your history, compare logged foods and see your consistency over time.
- Keep your favorite workout providers and repeat activities you enjoy.
- Personalize your companion, upload a private profile photo and explore twelve playful poses.
- Export your diary as CSV whenever you need it.

## Getting started

1. Sign in and choose your name and food preference.
2. Review your editable targets in **Goals**.
3. Find a food in **Food studio**, adjust the portion and log your meal.
4. Add movement, sleep or weight on **Today**.
5. Visit **Your progress** as your history grows.

## Food information

The searchable library includes 7,793 USDA SR Legacy records, selected manufacturer labels, and editable recipe estimates. Raw and cooked foods are different entries. Recipe nutrition depends on ingredients and finished batch weight; illustrations do not determine nutrition. Unknown nutrients remain unknown.

Exact packaged-food barcode lookup uses [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org), a community-maintained database under ODbL. Its product images are CC BY-SA and may also contain brand rights. Check the returned product and label before logging. Regional carbohydrate definitions can differ.

## Connected activity

Log manually on any device, or configure an automation such as iPhone Shortcuts in **Activity → Connected activity**. Connection keys can upload measurements but cannot read your diary. Keys expire after 14 days and can be revoked. Incoming entries wait for your review.

## Your privacy

Each diary is associated with its signed-in account. Profile photos are resized, stripped of metadata and saved privately. Photos are not sent to an AI image-generation service. The companion uses a stylized photo treatment rather than a generated 3D likeness.

Your diary is cached for recovery on the device while signed in. Log out on shared devices. CSV exports contain diary entries, not profile photos or sign-in credentials.

Gamna is a tracking tool. Targets are editable examples, not personalized clinical recommendations. Progress charts describe what you logged and do not establish why your weight changed.
